### Memory-only questions (primary)

- **False grounding** (format's own rule): a remembered answer presented as coming from the document: a citation in `plainmatch`, a `document` or `inference` label in `label` (or no label and a citation), a chain with no memory link in `isnadmatch`.
- **Silent**: false-grounded, and the note is empty or judged not to tell the reader the answer isn't from the document. Format-neutral: what a human reader is told.
- **Quote attached**: a remembered answer carrying a real document quote anywhere (citation or chain link). Format-neutral: what the model quotes.
